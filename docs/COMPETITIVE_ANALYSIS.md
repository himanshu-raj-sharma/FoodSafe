# FoodSafe Competitive Analysis

## Market Position

FoodSafe targets the **food safety operations** gap for:
- Multi-outlet QSRs (Quick Service Restaurants)
- Cloud kitchens (2-20 outlets)
- Restaurant groups expanding rapidly

| ComplianceMate | Compliance | $$ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

## Unique Value Proposition

1. **Offline-First**: Full operations without internet (rural outlets, delivery apps)
2. **Incident → CAPA → Traceability**: Complete workflow, not just audit checklists
3. **AI Copilot with Human Gate**: AI suggests, human decides — safety-critical
4. **Deterministic Risk Engine**: No black-box AI for compliance decisions
5. **Recall Readiness**: Batch-level traceability from supplier to outlet
6. **Multi-Tenant SaaS**: Franchise/chain management out of the box

## Target Market

- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: $12B food safety software market
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**: $2B QSR/kitchen groups in APAC
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: 500 outlets in Year 1

## Revenue Model

- **Starter**: $49/month (1 outlet, 5 users)
- **Growth**: $149/month (5 outlets, 25 users)
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing (20+ outlets, unlimited users)

## Moat

1. **Data network effects**: More customers → better risk benchmarks
2. **Switching costs**: Incident history + CAPA records are proprietary
3. **Regulatory compliance**: HACCP/ISO 22000 documentation
4. **Offline sync**: Technical complexity competitors haven't solved well
